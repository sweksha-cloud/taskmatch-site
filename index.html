How it works

You do nothing extra. No posting, no searching, no scheduling.

1

It notices

TaskMatch sits in your menu bar and picks up what you're on: a LeetCode problem, job applications, a CS course.

2

It finds someone

Every few minutes it looks for someone working on the same thing at the same moment.

3

You meet

One low-pressure popup. If you both accept, a video call opens for the two of you.

4

You stay connected

Add each other, and TaskMatch tells you when they're working on that topic again.

See it in action

A one-minute walkthrough of a real match.

Your activity stays on your laptop

Private pages and apps are filtered out first. Llama 3.2 runs on your Mac and turns the rest into one short sentence and a topic. That's all the server ever sees: no URLs, no page text, no titles.

Install

About a minute. You don't need an account.

  1. Download TaskMatch for Mac, open the .dmg and drag the app into Applications.
  2. Let macOS open it. TaskMatch isn't from the App Store yet, so macOS may say it's damaged or can't be opened. Run this once in Terminal, then open it again: 
    xattr -cr /Applications/work-match.app
  3. Click the globe in your menu bar, then Skip, try it as a guest.
  4. Try a match: pick a demo task to see the match popup and call flow without any setup.
Optional: match on your real activity
  1. Download the local AI model, Llama 3.2 3B (2.0 GB), or the lighter 1B version (0.8 GB), into: 
    ~/Library/Application Support/com.sultanalzoghaibi.workmatch/models/
  2. Download the Chrome extension and unzip it. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  3. Restart TaskMatch and turn on friend finding.
